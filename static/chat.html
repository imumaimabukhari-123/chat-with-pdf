Chat with your PDF

Ask questions and get answers from your document, with page numbers.

View code

1. Your document

How does it work?
  1. Your browser reads the PDF and splits it into small passages, remembering each passage's page.
  2. Each passage becomes an embedding, a list of numbers that captures its meaning (Google Gemini).
  3. For your question, a hybrid search (meaning + keywords) finds the most relevant passages.
  4. The AI answers using only those passages and cites the pages, so you can check every answer.

Your PDF is not saved anywhere. Close the tab and it's gone.
